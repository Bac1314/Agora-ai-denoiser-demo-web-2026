AI Noise Suppression Web demo

Agora Web SDK 4.24.9 · agora-extension-ai-denoiser@2.0.2

🎧 Wear headphones, everyone. If you use speakers, the other person's voice comes out of your speakers and back into your mic. Echo cancellation won't remove all of it. The denoiser then has to deal with that leftover voice: v2.0.2 tries to remove background voices, so it may cut or warble it. That makes the test unfair. "Hear myself" through speakers also causes feedback howl.

2. You (local)

Camera off

Off sends your mic with only the browser processing above. Turn it on and off while making noise to compare.

NSNG removes steady hum and sudden noise: keyboard, dogs, traffic, clatter. STATIONARY_NS removes only constant noise (fan, AC, hiss) and uses much less CPU.

AGGRESSIVE removes more noise but is more likely to damage the voice: clipped word endings, a "watery" sound, soft speech dropped.

Use LOW when delay matters more than noise removal.

Plays your own mic after processing, with a short delay. Headphones only.

Saves audio files from before and after processing. Send them to Agora support if you report an audio problem.

3. Remote users

No one else is here yet. Open the invite link on another device.

What you hear is the remote person's audio after their own denoiser settings. Your settings change what they hear from you.

How to test

  1. Two devices (laptop + phone, or two laptops), both on headphones. Use the same App ID and channel. Copy invite link fills both in.
  2. On device A, talk while making noise: type hard, crinkle paper, play café or traffic noise from a phone speaker next to the mic.
  3. Listen on device B while A switches AI noise suppression, Mode and Level. Changes apply live, with no rejoin.
  4. Testing alone? Join, put on headphones, tick Hear myself and switch settings while making noise.

Where the denoiser runs

Mic → browser AEC/ANS/AGC → AI denoiser → sent to the channel (and to "Hear myself"). The denoiser only works on the sender's mic. Each person controls what the others hear from them.

Background voice removal

v2.0.2 adds removal of background human voices (other people talking nearby). The docs list no setting for it: it is part of the v2.0.2 model and stays on while AI noise suppression is on. This is another reason for headphones: the other person's voice leaking from your speakers looks like a background voice.

Requirements and notes

  • Needs HTTPS (GitHub Pages is fine) or localhost. Desktop Chrome or Edge works best. The page warns you if your browser isn't supported.
  • The model runs in WebAssembly on your CPU. On slow machines the overload warning appears. Try STATIONARY_NS or LOW latency.
  • Tokens are tied to a channel name (and UID). If your project has an App Certificate, generate a temporary token for the exact channel you enter.
  • The invite link contains your App ID and channel, but not the token.
